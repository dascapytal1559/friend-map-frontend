# How it is wired

## Keeping private data out of commits

Nothing personal is committed: tests, fixtures and examples use made-up people only ([AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md) says how). The author's clones are guarded by the life archive's privacy guard, which stops a commit or a push that would publish a real name, id, number or host, and has a model read each commit once against this repository's policy; it lives there with the policy (see "The privacy guard" in the life archive's README) and is installed into a clone with the life archive's `npm run guard -- install friend-map --repo <clone>`. A clone without the life archive has no guard, so keep to made-up names. The demo is checked by the same guard on every build before it is deployed (see [Demo](demo.md)).

## The code

- `server/api.ts`: the small JSON API besides the archive's: `POST /api/usage`, `GET /api/settings`, and `GET /api/me` with `GET`, `PUT` and `DELETE /api/me/photo` for your photo on Orbits (`server/mePhoto.ts`). `server/jsonApi.ts` has the pieces it and `server/methodology.ts` are made of (the life archive's routes keep a copy of their own): reading a request's body, sending JSON, and refusing what another site's page sent.
- `server/check-settings.ts`: `npm run check-settings`, which checks the settings file (the people are the backend's to check).
- `server/settings.ts` reads `data/settings.json` for `GET /api/settings` and `npm run check-settings`; `src/settings.ts` has the keys, their defaults and checks, and the page's loader.
- `server/viewSettings.ts` keeps the saved view settings, `data/view-settings.json`, for `GET` and `PUT /api/view-settings`; `server/savedSettingsPlugin.ts` serves them into every page (and the demo's build); `src/savedSettings.ts` has which are saved and their values, and `src/savedStore.ts` the page's stores over them and their sending (see [Saved view settings](settings.md#saved-view-settings)).
- `server/usage.ts` and `server/usage-report.ts`: the usage record (`data/usage.jsonl`) and `npm run usage`. `src/usage.ts` is the page's side: what counts as a visit and as visible time.
- `server/main.ts`: friend-map's HTTP server: its API, the archive's routes through `server/methodology.ts` and the proxy, and Vite in middleware mode. `server/archiveProxy.ts` passes the page's archive requests to the backend and its answers back, streaming; `server/archiveClient.ts` reads the archive's facts from it (the score report, the Table's rows and the map's facts), keeping what carries an ETag.
- The backend is not here. The author's, the life archive (`~/Projects/life-archive`, see [Running](running.md)), has its own README, which maps its code: its `server/main.ts`, the archive (`server/archive/`), the connectors (`server/connectors/`), Generate's AI service (`server/ai.ts`), the CLI, the made-up exports and testing crowd (`server/fixtures/`) and the testing data.
- `server/pinnedFiles.test.ts`: the two files the life archive keeps pinned copies of, the same on both sides and drift-tested there: the contract (`src/archive.ts`) and the made-up names (`server/fixtures/madeUpNames.ts`, which every test takes its names from; its test holds every quoted name in the tests to them). Each imports nothing, so its copy stands on its own; a change to one is made here, committed, and copied there with the life archive's `npm run sync-contract`.
- `server/methodology.ts`: friend-map's own scoring over the archive's facts: it answers the backend's handshake with its own `scores` added (`GET /api/archive/contract`, taking the backend's time zone for its own counting), the Table (`GET /api/archive/table`) and a person's activity (`GET /api/archive/people/:id/activity`) with `src/demos/closeness.ts` and `src/activity.ts`, from the archive's score report (or, for a backend without one, its map's report) and rows read through an `ArchiveClient` (`server/archiveClient.ts`), and hands every other `/api/archive` route to the backend.
- The data contract (see [Bring your own backend](running.md#bring-your-own-backend)): `src/archive.ts` its types, version, capabilities and routes; `src/contract/` its shapes (`shape.ts`, `schema.ts`: what contract-check holds answers to, and the defaults the page fills in), the handshake (`handshake.ts`), the routes over an `ArchiveApi` in the page (`routes.ts`) and contract-check (`check.ts`); `server/contract-check.ts` and `server/contract-examples.ts` its commands; `docs/contract.md` and `docs/contract/` its pages; `src/pageQuery.ts` the query language run in the page; `src/BackendRefused.tsx` the page shown for a backend the page cannot use.
- `server/dataHome.ts`: where the data folder is and the archive's folder named to the backend, and where the backend answers (`FRIEND_MAP_DATA`, `FRIEND_MAP_ARCHIVE_FOLDER` and `FRIEND_MAP_ARCHIVE_URL`, each from the environment or `.env.local`, the old names `LIFE_ARCHIVE_DATA` and `LIFE_ARCHIVE_URL` read too; see [Running](running.md) and [The data folder](running.md#the-data-folder)), for every entry point.
- `src/main.tsx`: loads the map views (`src/demos/Demos.tsx`) and rewrites old addresses. `src/views.ts` lists every view; `src/ViewSwitcher.tsx` is the switcher on each.
- `src/PeoplePanel.tsx` (People, and the person panel, `PersonView`), `src/DraftBox.tsx` (the draft box, under the chat, saving with `src/drafts.ts`), `src/CatchUps.tsx` (hangouts, Add event's form and the logged calls in the chat, worded by `src/eventWords.ts`), `src/Composer.tsx` (sending, under the chat, off for now) with `src/liveState.ts` (how a live connector's state reads), `src/NameChips.tsx` (their names as chips beside the identicon, with `src/nameChips.ts`), `src/PersonParts.tsx` (tags, note, reaching out, accounts and fixes), `src/Timeline.tsx` (messages, a page at a time, new ones added as they come): the archive in the page, talking to it only through `archiveApi` (`src/archiveApi.ts`: the `ArchiveApi` interface, the data contract bound to the page, and `provideArchive`, which the page's entry calls once to say where the archive is, before it takes the backend's handshake; `src/archiveHttp.ts` is the server's routes behind it, and the static demo provides its own, see [Demo](demo.md); types in `src/archive.ts`), so a view can be replaced without touching the archive, and the archive without touching a view. friend-map's own server, apart from any backend (your settings and photo), is `appApi` (`src/appApi.ts`, over `src/appHttp.ts`, or the demo's `src/demo/app.ts`), provided the same way. `src/useArchive.ts` loads archive data into a component and reloads it whenever people change, and, for what shows counts, when new messages come in.
- `src/names.ts`: how a person is labelled, and the name choice: First name, First L., Full name or No name for the map, and No name except… everywhere (`useNames`); `src/NamesMenu.tsx` the choice, Name format, in the view's settings in the menu; and `src/shownNames.ts` whom No name except… names (the `show-name` tag).
- `src/demoMode.ts`: whether the page is the demo's (built as the static demo, or its address under `/demo`, `src/views.ts`) and where its views are; `src/pageArchive.ts` the archive the page uses, in it or not; `src/DemoMode.tsx` the note at the top of its map that says everyone in it is made up.
- `src/readOnly.ts`: whether the page can change anything; on a read-only page (the static demo's, and the app's demo at `/demo`) the person panel shows and changes nothing.
- `src/demo/`: the static demo (see [Demo](demo.md)): its page and entry (`index.html`, `main.tsx`), what Cloudflare Pages reads beside it (`public/`), the archive over the made-up crowd, the crowd's generator and words, the shape it is tuned to, and the Query bar's examples; `server/demo-shape.ts` writes the shape, and `server/check-demo.ts` (`npm run check-demo`) holds the built files to what may be published.
- `src/demos/`: the map views (see [The views](views.md)).
- `examples/demo-backend/`: the demo backend (see [Run friend-map with the demo backend](running.md#run-friend-map-with-the-demo-backend)), a backend of its own, which friend-map's code does not import: its schema (`schema.sql`), the made-up crowd written into it (`seed.ts`), a module of routes per capability, its HTTP server (`server.ts`) and its start (`main.ts`, `npm run demo-backend`); its README walks through it.

## Architecture

The map of the whole system, friend-map and its backend, is the life archive's Architecture page (see "Architecture" in the life archive's README); its drift test reads this repository's views, routes, files and the doc sections it names, so a change to them is followed there.
